# ClutchClip — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 5, 2026 · **Version 1.0**
**Provider:** Tremayne Saliim · **Contact:** tremayne.saliim@yahoo.com

---

## The short version
**Your videos, audio, transcripts, and clips never leave your computer. We never see, upload, or store your content.** All clipping and captioning runs **100% on your device, offline.** The app talks to our server only to activate your license and to occasionally confirm it hasn't been refunded.

## 1. What we collect — and when
The Software contacts our server **only** for license activation and an occasional license re-check. At those moments we receive:

| Data | What it is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **License key** | the key you bought on Gumroad (or a comp key) | confirm a valid purchase |
| **Hashed machine ID** | a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256 of your Windows MachineGUID + volume serial) — **not reversible** to anything personal, and **not** your name, MAC address, or location | enforce the 2-computer limit and re-bind your license |
| **App version** | e.g. `1.0.0` | compatibility |

That is the complete list.

## 2. What we do NOT collect
- **No video, audio, images, transcripts, or captions** — your content stays on your PC.
- **No usage analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no advertising IDs.**
- **No account and no email collected by the app** — the app has no login.
- **No background "phone-home"** beyond activation and the license re-check; the app otherwise runs fully offline.

## 3. Logs stay on your machine
The app writes diagnostic logs **only to your own computer** (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ClutchClip\logs\`). They are **never sent automatically.** If you need support, an in-app **"Export logs"** button lets *you* choose to zip and send them to us.

## 4. Payment
Purchases are processed by **Gumroad**, not by us. Your payment details go to Gumroad and are governed by **Gumroad's privacy policy** (linked on the product page). We never see your card information.

## 5. Storage and retention
Your license key and hashed machine ID are stored on our licensing server (**Cloudflare**) to maintain your activation and seat record. We keep them while your license is active. To request deletion, email us — note that deleting the record frees your seats and may require you to re-activate.

## 6. Children and location
ClutchClip is a creator tool not directed at children. The activation data described above is minimal and non-personal.
