### Why are my captions slow to generate?
Transcription runs entirely on your machine (nothing is uploaded). On a PC without a
graphics card it leans on the CPU and can take a few minutes — start a batch before a break
and we'll notify you when it's done.

### Where are my clips saved?
In `Documents\ClutchClip`, in a folder per job. From the Results screen use **Open clips
folder** or **Show in folder** to jump straight to them.

### How do I move ClutchClip to a new PC?
Install ClutchClip on the new PC, then in **Settings ▸ License** choose **Deactivate** on the
old PC (or free the seat) and activate on the new one. Your license covers a set number of
machines.

### Windows showed a SmartScreen warning when I installed.
New apps from independent developers haven't built up download reputation yet, so SmartScreen
warns by default. Click **More info → Run anyway**. The installer is the same file published
on our releases page.

### Can I activate without internet?
Activation needs a one-time online check. After that ClutchClip works fully offline —
clipping and captions never need a connection.

### I don't have a graphics card — will it still work?
Yes. ClutchClip falls back to CPU rendering automatically. It's slower than a GPU but
produces the same result; the app sets expectations and suggests clipping bigger batches
overnight.

### Does uninstalling and reinstalling reset my free trial?
No. The trial count is tied to your machine, not the install, so reinstalling won't give you
new trial clips. Upgrade with a license for unlimited, clean exports.
